“China”这个英文名从哪里来？

一个给外国人也能听懂的答案：它不是现代“Zhongguo”的音译，而是一个在南亚、中亚、西亚和欧洲之间流动了两千多年的外名。

Best Short Answer

“China”大概率来自古代外部世界对中国的称呼链：Cīna / Čīn / Ṣīn → China。

最常见的最终解释把源头追到“秦 Qin”，但严格说，学术上更稳妥的表述是：英语 China 直接来自近代欧洲语，近代欧洲语又继承了南亚和西亚的“Cina/Chin/Sin”一组名称；这些名称可能与秦有关，但不是百分之百可证。

English: The safest answer is not “China simply means Qin.” English China came through early modern European forms, especially Portuguese/Italian usage, which inherited older South Asian and West Asian names such as Cīna, Čīn, and Ṣīn. The Qin connection is plausible and influential, but the ultimate origin remains debated.

Ultimate source = Qin 秦中 / Medium

“秦说”最流行，也很合理；但早期文本年代、音变和替代地名假说使它不能被说成铁案。

传播路径：一个名字如何跨越大陆

The name is best understood as a chain of exonyms: names outsiders used in trade, diplomacy, geography, and later European sea routes.

核心链条

“China”是外名链条的晚期英语形态。

It is a late English form of a much older Eurasian exonym chain.

最大争议

最早的 Cīna/Chin 是否一定来自秦，仍有争议。

Whether the earliest Cīna/Chin forms certainly derive from Qin is still debated.

避免误解

不是“秦始皇把中国命名为 China”。

It does not mean Qin Shi Huang named the country "China."

关键时间线

Dates here separate the history of the Chinese polity from the history of foreign names. The two histories overlap, but they are not the same thing.

c. 9th-3rd c. BCE

秦作为西部国家兴起 / Qin as a western state

秦先是周代西部诸侯国，后逐步强大。若外部世界最早通过西北、西南或中亚贸易线听到某个“中国相关名称”，秦的地理位置和政治扩张使它成为合理候选。

Qin began as a western state before becoming imperial. Its western location and expansion make it a plausible source for a name carried outward through trade routes.

221-207 BCE

秦统一中国 / Qin imperial unification

秦朝时间很短，但统一制度影响巨大。流行说法认为 China 最终源于 Qin；这在音变和历史影响上有解释力，但不能简单等同为现代英语直接来自秦朝官方国名。

The Qin dynasty was brief but consequential. The popular derivation from Qin is plausible, yet it should not be simplified into a direct official naming story.

early centuries CE

Sinae / Thin / Seres 出现在希腊罗马地理传统

《厄立特里亚海航行记》、托勒密地理传统等记录了 Thin、Sinae、Seres 等远东名称。它们说明外部世界很早已有多个指向中国或中国边缘地区的名称系统。

Greco-Roman geography used forms such as Thin, Sinae, and Seres for lands at the eastern end of trade routes. These names show multiple naming traditions, not one neat line.

medieval period

Čīn / Ṣīn 在波斯、阿拉伯和中亚语境流通

波斯语、阿拉伯语和中亚贸易语境中，中国常被称为 Chin/Sin/Ṣīn 一类名称。这些形态与后来的欧洲 China 很容易互相连接。

Persian, Arabic, and Central Asian traditions used Chin/Sin-like names. These forms connect naturally to later European China/Cina forms.

13th c.

Marco Polo 用 Cathay 与 Manzi，不等于 China

马可波罗更常用 Cathay 指北方、Manzi/Mangi 指南方。Cathay 来自契丹 Khitan/Qidan，与 China 不是同源词。

Marco Polo's famous Cathay comes from Khitan/Qidan, not from China. It was a different exonym that survived in European usage for centuries.

1516-1563

葡萄牙海路文献推动 China 进入欧洲常用语

杜阿尔特・巴尔博萨、加西亚・德・奥尔塔等葡萄牙语语境把 China 作为国家/地区名固定传播。欧洲海上贸易使这个词超过 Cathay，成为近代欧洲主流称呼。

Portuguese maritime texts helped stabilize China as a European name. Sea trade made China more prominent than older Cathay in early modern Europe.

1555 onward

英语 China 见于 16 世纪 / English China appears in the 16th century

英语 China 的早期记录通常追到 Richard Eden 1555 年左右的航海和地理文本。随后该词同时发展出国家名、地区名和“来自中国的瓷器”之义。

English China is attested in mid-16th-century travel and geography writing, and later also produced the common noun china for porcelain.

主流秦说

为什么“秦说”有吸引力？

秦位于西部，最可能先被西方贸易网络接触；秦统一后政治声望巨大；Qin 与 Cīna/Chin 在古代转写中有可解释的音近关系。

Qin was western, politically prominent after unification, and phonetically close enough to Cīna/Chin-like forms to make the theory attractive.

年代问题

为什么不能说“已经铁证”？

一些梵文文本虽包含 Cīna，但文本成书、增补、传抄年代复杂。即使文本叙事很早，现存词形也未必能证明该词早于秦。

Indic references to Cīna occur in layered texts. A text's legendary or early setting does not automatically date every word in its surviving form.

替代假说

还有哪些竞争解释？

学者提出过日南/Rinan、滇/蜀/西南边地、荆/楚相关名称，或其他边疆族名来源。这些假说提醒我们：外名可能来自边贸接触点，而不一定来自中原王朝自称。

Alternatives derive the name from southern or southwestern frontier regions. They matter because exonyms often arise at contact zones, not in capital cities.

怎样向外国人解释

Use a clear analogy: exonyms are common. Germany/Deutschland and Greece/Hellas are easier parallels than a long philology lecture.

中文解释模板

“China”不是中国人今天自称“中国/Zhongguo”的音译。它是外国人在贸易和地理知识传播中形成的外名。这个名字可能最终和古代的秦 Qin 有关，但英语并不是直接从秦朝官方名称来的，而是经过梵文/印度语、波斯语/阿拉伯语以及葡萄牙语等中间环节，最后进入英语。类似地，德国人自称 Deutschland，英语却叫 Germany。

English explanation template

"China" is not a translation or romanization of modern Zhongguo. It is an exonym, a name used by outsiders. It probably belongs to an old Eurasian chain of names such as Cīna, Chin, and Sin, possibly connected to the ancient Qin state. English received it through early modern European usage, especially maritime trade. Think of it like Germany versus Deutschland: different historical naming systems.

一句话结论 / One-sentence answer: China is a foreign historical name for the Chinese world, probably linked to Qin through older Asian and European intermediaries, while Zhongguo is the Chinese self-name with its own separate history.

容易混淆的相关名称

These names are historically connected to how outsiders and insiders imagined China, but they are not interchangeable.

Zhongguo / 中国

中文自称

本义可理解为“中央之国/中央区域”，早期多指王畿、中心政治空间，后来逐渐成为国家与文明共同体名称。

A Chinese self-name, historically meaning central states/central realm before becoming a modern national name.

Sino-

学术前缀

Sino- 来自拉丁/希腊地理传统的 Sinae/Sina 形态，所以 Sino-American、Sinology 与 China 同属外名系统。

Sino- comes from Sina/Sinae-type classical forms and survives in Sinology and Sino-American.

Cathay

契丹来源

Cathay 来自 Khitan/Qidan 契丹，曾在欧洲指中国北方或整个中国，俄语 Kitai 也与此有关。

Cathay derives from Khitan/Qidan, a separate exonym, not a variant of China.

china 瓷器

小写普通名词

英语小写 china 指瓷器，是因为精美瓷器来自中国；不是国家名从瓷器来。

The common noun china for porcelain comes from the country name because porcelain was imported from China.

结论分层：专业表达建议

A careful explanation should not flatten uncertainty. Use three layers instead.

可以确定

China 是外名

它不是现代汉语“中国”的音译，而是欧洲从更早亚洲名称传统中继承的称呼。

It is an exonym inherited by Europe from older Asian naming traditions.

很可能

与秦有关

秦说有强解释力，适合向大众介绍，但最好加上“probably / likely”。

The Qin theory is plausible and widely used, but should be phrased as likely, not absolutely proven.

不要说

直接等同秦朝

不要说“中国英文名就是秦朝官方国名的直译”，也不要说“China 这个词由中国人自己创造”。

Avoid saying English China is a direct official Qin name or a Chinese-created English word.
